MANUFACTURING PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE KNOWLEDGE BASE

GENERAL PRINCIPLES

Machine-learning failure probability is a statistical signal and does not by itself prove that a machine has failed.

Predictive-maintenance alerts should be treated as decision-support information for qualified maintenance personnel.

Physical equipment should not be automatically stopped solely because an ML model produces a high-risk prediction.

HIGH TOOL WEAR

Tool wear represents accumulated tool usage.

High tool wear can increase cutting forces and may contribute to tool degradation or tool failure.

When high tool wear is detected:
- Inspect the cutting tool condition.
- Check whether the tool has reached its approved service interval.
- Inspect for abnormal wear or damage.
- Follow the approved tool inspection or replacement procedure.

HIGH TORQUE

Elevated torque can indicate increased cutting resistance, increased mechanical loading, tooling degradation, or abnormal operating conditions.

When high torque is detected:
- Inspect tooling and mechanical load conditions.
- Check for abnormal resistance.
- Review recent machine operating conditions.
- Follow approved maintenance procedures.

HIGH PROCESS TEMPERATURE

Elevated process temperature may indicate increased thermal stress or abnormal operating conditions.

When high process temperature is detected:
- Inspect cooling conditions.
- Check process operating parameters.
- Review recent temperature trends.
- Follow approved thermal inspection procedures.

HIGH AIR TEMPERATURE

Elevated air temperature can contribute to increased machine thermal conditions.

When high air temperature is detected:
- Check ambient operating conditions.
- Verify ventilation and cooling.
- Review temperature trends.
- Follow approved environmental operating procedures.

LOW ROTATIONAL SPEED

Unexpectedly low rotational speed may occur when the machine experiences increased mechanical resistance or abnormal operating conditions.

When low rotational speed occurs together with high torque:
- Prioritize inspection of mechanical loading.
- Inspect tooling and spindle conditions.
- Review operating parameters.

HIGH MECHANICAL LOAD

The combination of low rotational speed and high torque can indicate increased mechanical resistance or loading.

When high mechanical load is detected:
- Inspect tooling.
- Inspect spindle and mechanical components.
- Review recent machine operating conditions.
- Escalate to qualified maintenance personnel when required.

MULTIPLE WARNING CONDITIONS

Multiple simultaneous sensor warnings should receive higher maintenance priority than an isolated warning.

However, sensor warnings alone do not prove that equipment failure has occurred.

ML AND SENSOR EVIDENCE

The ML model provides an estimated probability of machine failure.

Sensor reason codes provide interpretable evidence about operating conditions.

The two signals should be considered together.

A low ML probability with multiple sensor warnings should trigger investigation rather than being treated as proof of failure.

A high ML probability with few visible sensor warnings should also be investigated because the model may identify nonlinear relationships that are not represented by simple threshold rules.

MAINTENANCE DECISION PRINCIPLES

LOW RISK:
Continue monitoring and collect additional operating data.

MEDIUM RISK:
Schedule a maintenance inspection and review relevant sensor trends.

HIGH RISK:
Prioritize an urgent maintenance inspection by qualified personnel.

The system should recommend investigation and maintenance actions. It should not automatically shut down physical equipment based only on model predictions.
