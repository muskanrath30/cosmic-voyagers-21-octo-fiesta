# Manufacturing Predictive Maintenance Knowledge Base
Multi-asset maintenance guidance used to ground the assistant's explanations.
Format: sections start with "## ", and a "Codes:" line lists the reason codes a
section applies to. Guidance is general engineering practice; always follow the
plant's approved procedures and the OEM manual.

## GENERAL PRINCIPLES
Codes: ALL
A machine-learning failure probability is a statistical signal and does not by
itself prove that a machine has failed. Alerts are decision-support information
for qualified maintenance personnel. Physical equipment must not be stopped
automatically because a model produced a high-risk prediction. A low model
probability together with several sensor warnings should trigger investigation.
A high model probability with few visible sensor warnings should also be
investigated, because the model can capture non-linear relationships that
simple threshold rules do not.

## MAINTENANCE DECISION PRINCIPLES
Codes: ALL
HIGH risk: arrange an inspection at the next safe opportunity and brief the
shift supervisor. MEDIUM risk: schedule an inspection within the planned
maintenance window and keep watching the trend. LOW risk: continue monitoring.
Record every inspection outcome against the alert so the model and thresholds
can be reviewed. If an inspection finds nothing, log it as a false alarm; if it
finds a defect, log the failure mode.

## CNC MACHINE TOOL - HIGH TOOL WEAR
Codes: HIGH_TOOL_WEAR
Tool wear is accumulated cutting time. High tool wear raises cutting forces and
can lead to tool breakage or poor surface finish. Check the tool against its
approved service interval, inspect the cutting edge for chipping or flank wear,
and follow the approved tool-change procedure.

## CNC MACHINE TOOL - HIGH TORQUE AND MECHANICAL LOAD
Codes: HIGH_TORQUE, HIGH_MECHANICAL_LOAD, LOW_ROTATIONAL_SPEED
Elevated torque, especially together with reduced rotational speed, indicates
increased cutting resistance or mechanical loading. Inspect tooling and the
spindle, review feed and speed settings, and check for obstructions or
lubrication problems. Prioritise this inspection when both conditions occur
together.

## CNC MACHINE TOOL - TEMPERATURE
Codes: HIGH_PROCESS_TEMPERATURE, HIGH_AIR_TEMPERATURE
Elevated process or ambient air temperature increases thermal stress. Inspect
coolant flow and condition, check ventilation and cooling, and review the recent
temperature trend. A process temperature that rises faster than the ambient
temperature points to a machine-side cause rather than the environment.

## METRO COMPRESSOR - PRESSURE LOSS
Codes: LOW_SYSTEM_PRESSURE, RESERVOIR_PRESSURE_MISMATCH
Low pneumatic-panel pressure (TP2/TP3), or a reservoir pressure that no longer
follows the panel pressure, is a typical signature of an air leak in the
air-production unit. Inspect the air-dryer towers, the separator filter
discharge, pipework joints and the intake valves. Check whether the compressor
is running continuously to make up for lost pressure. Escalate to depot
maintenance if the deficit persists across duty cycles.

## METRO COMPRESSOR - ELECTRICAL AND THERMAL
Codes: HIGH_MOTOR_CURRENT, HIGH_OIL_TEMPERATURE
Higher motor current together with rising oil temperature can indicate increased
mechanical loading, a developing oil leak or degraded lubrication. Inspect the
oil level and condition, check motor phase-current balance and review recent
duty cycles. A sustained rise in both warrants inspection before the next
extended service period.

## METRO COMPRESSOR - DRYER AND PRESSURE SWITCH
Codes: DRYER_DISCHARGE_ANOMALY, LOW_PRESSURE_SWITCH_ACTIVE
An abnormal dryer discharge pressure or an active low-pressure switch means the
air-drying cycle may not be completing. Inspect the solenoid valves and pressure
switch and verify the tower cycling logic. Repeated low-pressure switch
activation can starve downstream brake and door systems.

## TURBOFAN - SENSOR DEVIATIONS
Codes: S2_HIGH, S2_LOW, S3_HIGH, S3_LOW, S4_HIGH, S4_LOW, S7_HIGH, S7_LOW, S8_HIGH, S8_LOW, S9_HIGH, S9_LOW, S11_HIGH, S11_LOW, S12_HIGH, S12_LOW, S13_HIGH, S13_LOW, S14_HIGH, S14_LOW, S15_HIGH, S15_LOW, S17_HIGH, S17_LOW, S20_HIGH, S20_LOW, S21_HIGH, S21_LOW
Several sensor channels outside their healthy operating band indicate developing
engine degradation, most often in the high-pressure compressor or the fan.
Compare against the fleet baseline, trend the deviation over successive cycles,
and plan a borescope inspection of compressor blades and the bleed-valve and
exhaust gas path if the deviation persists.

## TURBOFAN - REMAINING USEFUL LIFE
Codes: S2_HIGH, S3_HIGH, S4_HIGH, S7_LOW, S11_HIGH, S12_LOW, S15_HIGH, S20_LOW, S21_LOW
An estimated remaining useful life (RUL) is a planning aid with uncertainty,
particularly far from failure. Use it to schedule the shop visit, not to
declare an engine unfit. A short RUL estimate combined with several deviating
channels justifies bringing the inspection forward.

## BEARING - VIBRATION
Codes: HIGH_DRIVE_END_VIBRATION, HIGH_FAN_END_VIBRATION
High RMS vibration at the drive-end or fan-end accelerometer indicates
increased bearing or shaft loading. Check mounting, alignment and balance,
inspect lubrication, and compare with the previous baseline for the same
machine.

## BEARING - IMPULSIVE FAULT SIGNATURE
Codes: IMPULSIVE_VIBRATION_KURTOSIS, HIGH_CREST_FACTOR
High kurtosis or a high crest factor means spiky, impulsive vibration, which is
characteristic of early localised defects such as outer-race, inner-race or
rolling-element damage. Inspect the bearing, check lubrication and plan a
replacement before the defect propagates.

## NO MAJOR ANOMALY
Codes: NO_MAJOR_SENSOR_ANOMALY
No significant sensor anomaly was detected. Continue normal monitoring and data
collection. No immediate maintenance action is required.
